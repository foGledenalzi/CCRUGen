<objective>
Fix the three main-thread hazards that large bases expose outside the renderer: the base picker snapping its numeral back while the
worker computes (PATTERNS hazard 5, research P19), the Selection panel's O(k^2) order pass and unbounded list at n = 4000 (hazard 7,
P5), and the URL codec building a numogram for a huge base (P6, `shareParams.ts`).

Purpose: D-07's computing state lives in the picker; REN-03 "the UI never freezes" also covers the panel and the URL parser.

[default] decisions recorded here (review at verification):
- The role=status carrier is a persistent sr-only span (checker recommendation 1); the visible summary span is replaced in place by `Computing base {n}...` and carries `data-computing="true"` with no role, so the announcement is not doubled.
- SELECTION_LIST_MAX = 200 entries at the Canvas and headless tiers, followed by `and {M} more selected` (10px, gray, data-post-baseline); no cap at the SVG tier (at most about 800 entries at base 200, unchanged behaviour).
- New picker props are optional (default: requestedBase = base, computing = false, announcement = ''), so NumogramClient keeps compiling until 06-11 wires them.
Output: three modified source files, one new lib module and three test files.
</objective>

<objective>
Integrate the Canvas tier and the worker into the viewer: the render-tier decision, the latest-wins request path with deferred URL
hydration, the batched swap, the lazy headless numogram, the canvas branch, the new over-limit message, and the deliberate rewrite of
the e2e specs that assumed the old 202-4000 fallback.

Purpose: ROADMAP criteria 1, 3 and 4 become observable in the browser (REN-02, REN-03). Research P2 (sync commit or older result
overwriting a newer state), P3 (layout change orphaning a pending base switch), P6 (main-thread createNumogram call sites), P7
(the first render is the base-10 SVG), PATTERNS hazard 6 (useLayoutTween call order) and 8 (rewrite, never skip) are owned here.

[default] decisions recorded here (orchestrator decisions 5, 8 and planner choices, review at verification):
- RECORDED DEVIATION from D-06's literal wording (accepted by the plan checker; record it under requirements in the 06-11 SUMMARY): main-thread `g`, `view` and zone labels are still built at the Canvas tier (0.26 ms + 11 ms at n = 4000; InfoDisplay, Regions and Demons read them synchronously). The intent of D-06 is kept: the worker builds the numogram, summary, text, layout, routes and scene, and no layout or routes are computed on the main thread at the Canvas tier.
- Headless: `g` is null in NumogramClient; the context value exposes `g` through a getter that calls `createNumogram(base)` (LRU-cached by the engine) only when read, i.e. when the Demons overlay opens or a demon focus is pinned; a one-time delay of about 0.6 s at 2^26 is accepted. A demon focus from the URL at a headless base builds it once at the swap.
- `tier=canvas` or `tier=headless` together with a base-10 preset layout renders SVG (UI-SPEC decision 6, extended to headless; diagnostic only).
- URL hydration for a worker-path base keeps the base-10 SVG as "the last result", stashes the base-shaped URL state and applies it in the swap; `urlSyncReady` turns true only after the swap or a failure, so the URL keeps `?base=` while pending.
- On a worker failure the displayed state is kept (layout, packer and labels revert to the displayed result) and the picker's refusal slot shows `Couldn't compute base {n}. Showing base {lastValid}.` or the no-worker line.
- Failure and success copy dead ends are intended (checker recommendation 2): `To draw it, use headless export.` points at Phase 8; do not add a link, button or retry.
Output: the integrated viewer, the switched over-limit component, and five rewritten e2e specs plus Canvas-aware helpers.

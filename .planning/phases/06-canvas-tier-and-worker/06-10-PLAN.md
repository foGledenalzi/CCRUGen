<objective>
Build the Canvas tier's DOM shell, `CanvasDiagram`, plus a small pure glue module, wiring the scene, the draw module, the pick index,
the keyboard model and the pointer machine into one focusable, touch-capable canvas.

Purpose: ROADMAP criteria 1 and 2 (same features as the SVG tier; correct picks; labels by level of detail on zoom), D-05, D-08, D-09.
Follow the `DemonMatrix.tsx` shell (role=application, one Tab stop, native non-passive wheel listener, pointer capture, 12 px tooltip
offset, aria-live region, data attributes for e2e); copy the shell, not its raster.

[default] decisions recorded here (orchestrator decision 3 and checker recommendation 4, review at verification):
- Research A2: during a continuous gesture (wheel burst, drag, pinch) the cached layer stack is stretched with a CSS transform and re-rastered after the gesture ends (wheel idle WHEEL_COMMIT_DELAY_MS = 120 ms or pointer up) in the next animation frame; discrete zooms (toolbar, keys, Fit, resize, DPR change) re-raster at once.
- The callout offset 12 px is a named exception (`CALLOUT_OFFSET_PX`), matching the demon matrix tooltip.
- `data-canvas-state` is 'pending' until the current scene has painted once and nothing is pending (the UI-SPEC lists only ready|pending; first paint counts as pending).
- In Demon focus mode, Enter on a zone announces the plain zone text (no selected/not selected suffix), since the click opens focus instead of toggling selection.
Output: one pure module, one component and two test files.
</objective>

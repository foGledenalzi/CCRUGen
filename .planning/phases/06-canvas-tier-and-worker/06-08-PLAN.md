<objective>
Write the Canvas drawing module: pure functions that paint the scene's layers and the interaction overlay through an injected 2D
context, so every parity rule from the UI-SPEC is unit-testable with a recording context (no canvas package, no jsdom).

Purpose: D-04 (edges follow the table, on demand for a selection), D-05 (all four layouts, focus chords), D-08's non-colour cues
carried to Canvas, Phase 3 D-07 level of detail.

[default] decisions recorded here (orchestrator decision 3 and checker recommendation 5, review at verification):
- Research A3: context dimming is the per-kind edge layer's CSS opacity (`edgeLayerOpacity`), edges are painted at alpha 1 on their layer (isolate-dimmed edges at contextAlpha / ambientAlpha), and highlighted plus on-demand edges are drawn on the overlay. A repaint per hover would cost 29-249 ms at n = 4000 on the shipped profile.
- Fidelity downgrade: above `HIGHLIGHT_FULL_FIDELITY_MAX = 400` highlighted edges the overlay draws plain batched strokes at highlighted alpha (no graded dots, arrowheads, junction circles, underlays or edge text) and `drawOverlay` returns `{ highlightedEdges, fidelity: 'plain' }`; 06-10 exposes it as data attributes and 06-12 logs it.
- On-demand edges respect the user's Layers toggles (a layer switched off draws nothing, not even on demand); they override only the density rule (gateLayerMode 'off').
- Canvas simplifications (UI-SPEC "Fidelity tiers"): ambient syzygies are a dotted round-cap line, ambient currents a solid line with an arrowhead only when their stem is at least 24 CSS px long and a junction circle only at label level of detail; partial-selection gradients of the SVG tier are not reproduced: a partially selected edge draws as a solid stroke at its normal alpha. This is recorded as UI-SPEC "Decisions Made Here" item 7 (Canvas highlighted edges use a solid stroke; partial-selection gradients are SVG-tier only) and listed for review in 06-15.

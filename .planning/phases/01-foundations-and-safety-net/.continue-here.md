<context>
The project is a numogram construction and visualization tool. The math is verified: for even base n, syzygy pairs sum to n-1, currents send hi::lo to hi-lo (a permutation of pairs, so regions are its cycles), gates send zone k to the in-base digital root of T(k), demons are all C(n,2) zone pairs. Multi-Torque bases are the norm. The hard parts are rendering at high bases and keeping the base-10 output byte-identical while refactoring, which is why Phase 1 freezes an oracle before any code moves.
